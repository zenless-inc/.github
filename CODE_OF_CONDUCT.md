# Code of Conduct

Zenless is built by and for people who like calm, well-made tools. We want the community around it to feel the same way.

## Our standards

Please:

- be kind, patient and welcoming, especially to newcomers;
- assume good intent, and ask before you assume;
- give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, and keep criticism about the work, not the person;
- respect differing opinions, experiences and levels of expertise;
- take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

Not acceptable:

- harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks;
- discriminatory jokes or language, or unwelcome sexual attention;
- publishing someone's private information without their permission;
- trolling, deliberately derailing discussions, or spamming;
- anything else a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all Zenless spaces: repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions and any other channel
run by the project. It also applies when you represent the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainers privately through the
[zenless-inc organization](https://github.com/zenless-inc) on GitHub. Mention "Code of Conduct" in your message.
Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, code and issues that break this code. They may also warn, or
temporarily or permanently ban, anyone whose behaviour they judge harmful. Responses scale with how serious and how
repeated the behaviour is. Maintainers who don't follow the code in good faith may lose their role.
